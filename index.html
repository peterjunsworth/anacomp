The problem

Four pressures on agency records

Records programs are being asked to do more, faster, with files they can’t fully see, search, share or serve.

Visibility

Unmapped file estates

Years of file shares and cloud folders, with no count of what is there or how much is duplicate.

Paper

Paper that can’t be searched

Boxes and file rooms hold records that staff must pull by hand and cannot serve digitally.

Privacy

Sensitive data in the open

PII sits inside unstructured documents, so nothing can be shared until someone reads every page.

Access

Accessibility obligations

Section 508 and ADA Title II expect documents that work with assistive technology, not image-only PDFs.

The offering

Five stages, one analytics layer

Six services. Each one is complete on its own, and each one’s output is the next one’s input.

Stage 1 · Discover

Know what you hold before you move it

Congruity360 runs a shallow scan across file systems and cloud file repositories and returns the facts a records owner needs first.

What you receive

  • File counts, sizes and types by location
  • Duplicates identified for de-duplication
  • A sized scope for any redaction or remediation that follows

Powered by

Congruity360

Stands alone when

You’re planning a cloud migration, a storage clean-up or an AI rollout and need an inventory first.

Leads to

Redact for sensitive files; Remediate for public-facing ones.

Stage 2 · Digitize

Bring paper into the digital flow

Anacomp scanning services ingest paper documents and deliver indexed images that every later stage can work on.

What you receive

  • Paper scanned and indexed to your scheme
  • Digital files delivered to your repository or straight into the pipeline
  • One digital starting point for redaction, accessibility and release

Powered by

Anacomp scanning services

Stands alone when

You have a paper backlog, a file-room closure or an electronic-records mandate to meet.

Leads to

Remediate, so scans become accessible data; Redact before anything is released.

Stage 3 · Redact

Remove what must not be released

Anacomp redaction services remove PII and sensitive information from unstructured data, run as workflows on Hyperscience.

What you receive

  • PII and sensitive content removed to your rules
  • Redacted copies ready to release or share
  • One process for scanned paper and born-digital files

Powered by

Hyperscience workflows, operated by Anacomp

Stands alone when

You face a records-request backlog, a litigation production or a data-sharing agreement.

Leads to

Release through the portal; Remediate so the redacted copy is also accessible.

Stage 4 · Remediate

Turn any document into accessible data

ADA takes PDFs, Excel, PowerPoint, Word and image files, transcribes them, saves the content as data and republishes it in accessible form.

What you receive

  • Accessible HTML for the web
  • Accessibly tagged documents for download
  • Content captured as data, ready for search and reuse

Powered by

Anacomp ADA

Stands alone when

You have an accessibility audit, complaint or mandate and a library of untagged documents.

Leads to

Release, to deliver accessible copies; Digitize, for paper still outside the library.

Ask the Document Powered by Botcopy

Put an accessible assistant beside the HTML that ADA produces, on your own site or portal. Residents ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from the document whenever needed.

Stage 5 · Release

Give the public a verified front door

The Anacomp portal lets members of the public, authenticated through ID.me, request documents that pass through the other stages before release.

What you receive

  • Identity-verified document requests from the public
  • Agency tools for requests, processing and client management
  • A release step that can route through Digitize, Redact and Remediate first

Powered by

Anacomp portal with ID.me sign-in

Stands alone when

You need a request portal now and already have your own redaction or accessibility process.

Leads to

Every other stage: each request the portal takes in is work for them.

Across every stage · Analyze

Ask your records program anything

Insignal builds a data warehouse from what the other stages produce and lets staff ask questions in plain English, then turn the answers into dashboards and alerts.

What you receive

  • Estate dashboards from Discover scans: growth, duplicates, risk
  • Operations dashboards: request volumes, turnaround, backlog
  • Questions answered in plain English, in the browser or in Teams and Slack

Powered by

Insignal, self-hosted in your own cloud

Stands alone when

Leaders need to report on records work, or want answers from data that ADA has extracted.

Leads to

Discover, to feed it the estate; Release, to measure every request.

End to end

A public records request, start to finish

From a verified request to an accessible, redacted release, in one chain of custody.

  1. 1

    Request

    A member of the public verifies identity with ID.me and asks for documents in the portal.

  2. 2

    Locate

    Agency staff pick up the request in the portal and find the records, on paper or in file stores.

  3. 3

    Digitize

    Paper records are scanned and indexed.

  4. 4

    Redact

    PII and sensitive content are removed in Hyperscience workflows.

  5. 5

    Remediate

    ADA produces accessible HTML and a tagged document for download.

  6. 6

    Release

    The agency approves and releases the documents to the requester in the portal.

?

Ask the Document, powered by Botcopy. Rather than scrolling through pages to find an answer, requesters just ask, and get a plain-language reply drawn from the document itself.

↷

Records skip what they don’t need. Born-digital files bypass Digitize, and files with nothing sensitive bypass Redact.

◎

Insignal, throughout. Every request is tracked, so staff can ask about volumes, turnaround and backlog in plain English.

How it is sold

Two ways to buy

Option A

The full pipeline

  • One contract and one service level across every stage, with Insignal reporting on all of it
  • Records move from stage to stage without leaving Anacomp custody
  • The portal is the public’s front door and the agency’s work queue

Best for Agencies with steady request volume and a mix of paper and digital records.

Option B

Any stage on its own

  • A separate statement of work per stage, sized to one backlog or mandate
  • Files in, files out: each stage works alongside your existing systems
  • Each stage’s output is the next stage’s input, so adding one later needs no rework

Best for Clients with one pressing problem, or with tools that already cover other stages.
