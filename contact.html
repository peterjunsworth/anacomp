Questions

Before you get in touch

Do we have to buy the whole pipeline?

No. Every stage is complete on its own and works alongside your existing systems: files in, files out. Because each stage’s output is the next stage’s input, adding another later needs no rework.

What does the 30-day pilot include?

Week 1: Discover scans one repository you choose. Weeks 2–3: a sample set of pages runs through Redact and Remediate for your review. Week 4: a portal sandbox walks one request from ID.me sign-in to release, with the pilot reported in Insignal. It ends with a scoped proposal.

How is each stage priced?

Each stage has its own unit of sale: per terabyte for Discover, per image for Digitize, per page for Redact and Remediate, an annual fee plus request tiers for Release, and per seat for Analyze. Bundles are discounted against the sum of their stages.

Do our records leave our control?

On the full pipeline, records move from stage to stage without leaving Anacomp custody, so there are no hand-offs between vendors to secure. Insignal is self-hosted in your own cloud.

Which technology is inside?

Congruity360 for Discover, Hyperscience workflows for Redact, ID.me for identity in the Release portal, and Insignal for Analyze. Anacomp integrates and operates them all.
