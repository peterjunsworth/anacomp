U.S. Navy · Federal health records

Digitizing 65 million pages of Navy service treatment records

  • Digitize
Challenge
A 2013 White House mandate required service treatment records received by the Department of Veterans Affairs to be electronic, with paperless transfer from January 2014. Navy records had to be separated by service member as they were digitized.
Approach
Anacomp assessed the collection and ran a semi-automated scanning and indexing process with chain-of-custody tracking, separating each service member’s record.
Outcome
65 million pages of paper records converted. Since 2014, Anacomp has processed millions of service treatment records at over 99.7% accuracy.

Federal health records · HIPAA

Catching misfiled patient records before they become violations

  • Discover
  • Redact
  • Release
Challenge
A misfiled page in a service treatment record is a HIPAA violation, can affect a veteran’s disability benefits, and triggers a 100-year retention requirement for the whole batch. A 2022 policy prioritized fast remediation.
Approach
Anacomp’s D3TECT indexes the personal identifiers in every digitized record, compares them with the known patient identity and flags mismatches for human review. R3DACT then automates PHI and PII redaction for release-of-information requests.
Outcome
Manual review time cut by more than 80%, false positives by 50% and false negatives by 66%. Redaction review time cut by about 40%. Projected personnel cost avoidance of $2.5M–$4M a year per service branch.
