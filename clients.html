County · Paper to Accessible

Closing a file room with an accessibility duty

  • Digitize
  • Remediate
Challenge
“We have to close the file room.” Decades of paper had to go digital and meet ADA Title II.
Approach
Paper was scanned and indexed to the county’s scheme, then ADA turned every image into accessible HTML and tagged documents.
Outcome
Add the measured result here: boxes digitized, pages remediated, floor space reclaimed.

  • Redact

Approach

Outcome
