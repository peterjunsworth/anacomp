Introducing the Anacomp Information Lifecycle

AnnouncementNovember 2026· 4 min read

Public agencies are under four pressures at once: unmapped file estates, paper that can’t be searched, sensitive data in the open, and accessibility obligations. Today we’re bringing our answer together into one pipeline.

The Anacomp Information Lifecycle takes records from raw state to public release. It runs five stages with one analytics layer across all of them, and agencies can buy it end to end or one stage at a time.

Five stages, one analytics layer

  1. Discover. Congruity360 scans file systems and cloud repositories for counts, sizes and duplicates.
  2. Digitize. Anacomp scanning services bring paper into the same flow as digital files.
  3. Redact. Anacomp redaction, run as workflows on Hyperscience, removes PII and sensitive content.
  4. Remediate. Anacomp ADA turns any format into data, accessible HTML and tagged documents.
  5. Release. The Anacomp portal, with ID.me sign-in, lets verified requesters ask and agencies manage and release.

Across every stage, Insignal builds a data warehouse from what the pipeline produces. Staff ask questions in plain English, in the browser or in Teams and Slack, and turn the answers into dashboards and alerts.

A records request, start to finish

A member of the public verifies their identity with ID.me and asks for documents in the portal. Agency staff pick up the request and find the records, on paper or in file stores. Paper is scanned and indexed, sensitive content is redacted, and ADA produces an accessible copy. The agency approves the release in the portal.

A record skips any stage it doesn’t need. Born-digital files bypass Digitize, and files with nothing sensitive bypass Redact.

Two ways to buy

The full pipeline gives one contract and one service level across every stage, with records that never leave Anacomp custody. Any stage on its own is sized to one backlog or mandate and works alongside the systems you already have. Because each stage’s output is the next stage’s input, adding one later needs no rework.

Start with a 30-day pilot

In week one we scan one repository. In weeks two and three, a sample set of pages runs through Redact and Remediate for your review. In week four, a portal sandbox walks one request from ID.me sign-in to release, all reported in Insignal. The pilot ends with a scoped proposal: the full pipeline, or the stages that proved their value.
